I logged the hours for building the test motors so here's the info for that. There is no CAD or files for this. This is NOT an official motor recipe and is VERY experimental. Make at your own risk.

## Materials
- PVC Pipe
- Ammonium Perchorate Powder
- Aluminum Powder
- Silicone
- Kitty Litter

## Instructions
- Cut the PVC Pipe into a 4 inch section.
- Grind up a half cup of Kitty Litter in an old blender or with a mortar and pestle. The essential ingredient is the Bentonite Clay which can be very strong.
- Put some kitty litter powder into the bottom of the pvc pipe and compress it with a ramrod and mallet until you have a 1cm layer that is incompressible.
- Separately, prepare the rocket motor fuel. Mix the ammonium perchlorate (50% by weight), aluminum (25%) and silicone (25%).
- Carefully put the fuel into the motor casing, being VERY CAREFUL not to leave any air bubbles. Make an 1/8 in core down the middle (easily done with a pencil)
- Fill the rest of the motor with kitty litter and compress it with the ramrod and mallet.
- Drill an eighth inch hole in the clay as the nozzle. Make sure to clear all clay from the core.

## Launch instructions
- Use the ESTES model rocket starters to light it. Make sure to have at least a 15ft radius around it in case of catastrophic rapid unscheduled disassembly.
